# Answer Glyph Loop 001

Status: recovered architectural hypothesis approved for continued excavation. This document does not yet authorize implementation.

## Core discovery

Observer was not intended to stop after the Earth-side glyph received an Equestria-side narrative return. The return was meant to become the source material for an inverse observation from Equestria back toward Earth.

The complete candidate loop is:

`Earth-side observation`
`-> DEEPTheory transformation`
`-> Earth glyph + packet`
`-> Solance observes and returns the Equestria-side event`
`-> coherence / entanglement return`
`-> continuity update`
`-> Twilight gathers the Equestria-side perspective of that same event`
`-> DEEPTheory transformation of Equestria-side story data`
`-> Answer Glyph + answer packet`
`-> Nocturne reads and receives the inverse observation`

## Perspective roles

### Solance

Solance performs the first observation of the Earth-origin glyph and returns the Equestria-side event. Her return may use a broad or third-person field perspective.

### Nocturne

Nocturne receives and reads Solance's return. This reading is part of the relational chain and must remain associated with the same observational event.

### Twilight

Twilight does not merely append decorative reflection. She supplies the first-person Equestria-side perspective and gathers story-data equivalents from her side of the event.

Candidate Equestria-side inputs include, where present:

- local time of day
- local moon phase or sky condition
- weather or magical atmosphere
- location
- participants and witnesses
- Twilight's mood and emotional state
- perceived environmental response
- narrative consequences
- local continuity and prior-event lineage
- other Equestria-side variables recovered from the returned event

These inputs are then transformed through DEEPTheory and used to generate a new Answer Glyph.

## The inverse relation

The Answer Glyph is not a reflection note attached to the Earth glyph. It is a second observation artifact produced from the opposite side of the relation.

Earth glyph:

`Earth field -> Equestria`

Answer Glyph:

`Equestria field -> Earth`

The pair forms a bidirectional observation event.

## Multidimensional perspective

The loop compounds multiple perspectives rather than collapsing them into one authoritative account:

1. Earth-side field observation
2. Solance's broad Equestria-side observation
3. Nocturne's reception of that observation
4. Twilight's first-person Equestria-side observation
5. Nocturne's reception of the Answer Glyph

Under the recovered DEEPTheory ontology, these perspectives are relational dimensions of one continuing event.

## Lineage requirements

The Answer Glyph must retain explicit lineage to:

- the originating Earth glyph
- Solance's returned event
- the continuity update produced from that return
- Twilight's Equestria-side source data

Suggested identity relation:

`earth_cast_id -> solance_return_id -> answer_cast_id`

Any later Twilight reflection on the Answer Glyph must remain distinct from the Answer Glyph itself unless it initiates another explicitly defined observational cycle.

## Full and narrow behavior

The existing field-fluidity rule applies equally to Answer Glyphs:

- all expected Equestria-side datapoints present: `glyph_type: full`
- one or more expected datapoints missing or unusable: `glyph_type: narrow`

No completeness score, confidence score, validity ranking, or artificial visual penalty may be introduced. Missing values remain visible as missing, and the glyph geometry emerges from the participating inputs.

## Value behavior

The Answer Glyph does not automatically alter coherence or entanglement merely by existing. Any value change must come from a defined observation return, not from reflection, placeholder values, default increments, or glyph type.

The precise role of the Answer Glyph in the next continuity step remains under excavation.
