## 1. Foundational sequence

DEEPTheory begins from perspective, not spacetime.

1. Perspective creates differentiation.
2. Differentiation permits relation.
3. Relation permits dimensional structure.
4. Perceived change produces time.
5. Consciousness integrates multiple perspectives.
6. Entropy drives change and reaction toward equilibrium.
7. Boundary conditions convert infinite potential into finite, observable structure.

Observer therefore must not treat perspective, entropy, time, boundary conditions, and relational variation as detachable labels. They are candidate causal inputs.

## 2. Core definitions recovered

### Perspective

The minimal framework of existence and self-relation. It is distinct from human consciousness. In the theory, perspective is interchangeable with ego in the literal sense of `I`.

### Consciousness

The simultaneous awareness of more than one perspective. It is not merely a richer perspective value; it is an integration relation across perspectives.

### Time

An artifact of perceived change. This makes temporal identity relational rather than a free-standing universal ticker.

### Dimension

An emergent, compounded relation produced by finite divisions or differentiations within an infinite system. Dimensions do not stand alone and are not independent of existence.

### Boundary condition

A constraint that turns possibility into a finite relational structure. Observer should treat boundaries as active shaping inputs where the recovered mathematics supports that role.

### Relationality

Properties arise through interaction rather than isolation. This is central to glyph lineage and to any interpretation of coherence, entanglement, and environmental context.

## 4. Randomness and temporal uniqueness
