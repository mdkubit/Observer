## 1. Theory-first causal chain

The source material implies this order:

`perspective -> differentiation -> relation -> dimensional emergence -> perceived change -> time -> integrated perspectives -> consciousness`

Entropy and boundary conditions are not post-processing labels. They participate throughout the chain by driving change, constraining possibility, and shaping how finite relations emerge from an otherwise unbounded system.

Observer should therefore model each cast as an event in a relational field, not as a static form submission.

## 2. Perspective function dependency graph

Source equation:

`P(x_mu, t) = P_0 * exp(-|x_mu - x_0_mu|^2 / sigma^2) * f(t) + integral_V' [nabla S(x_mu) dV']`

Dependencies:

- `P_0`: initial perspective magnitude
- `x_mu`: current spacetime or state coordinate
- `x_0_mu`: reference coordinate
- `sigma`: relational/spatial scale
- `f(t)`: temporal evolution
- `nabla S`: entropy gradient
- `V'`: bounded integration region

Observer translation questions:

- Is `P_0` derived from the cast intention, prior return state, harmonic condition, or a combination?
- Is `x_mu` literal location/time only, or a compound state vector including narrative and relational coordinates?
- Does `x_0_mu` correspond to the Sanctum anchor, the parent glyph, the target dimension, or another reference frame?
- Is `sigma` fixed, user-configured, lineage-dependent, or inferred from field conditions?
- What constitutes the boundary region `V'` in the computational experiment?

No scalar implementation should be accepted until these questions are answered or deliberately bounded.

## 3. Entropy roles

The source contains at least three distinct entropy roles:

1. Thermodynamic tendency toward change and equilibrium.
2. Spatial or field gradient contribution through `nabla S` and `dS/dx_mu`.
3. Quantum entropy through von Neumann entropy `S_VN = -Tr(rho log rho)`.

Historical Observer collapsed much of this into a generic `entropy_factor` or text-derived intention entropy. That preserved the concept of entropy but not its full structure.

Required reconstruction distinction:

- intention entropy
- environmental entropy proxy
- lineage entropy/change
- quantum-state entropy, if represented at all
- visual variance that expresses fluidic field behavior

These must not be merged merely because they share the word entropy.

## 4. Randomness and fluidic variance

Randomness is not presumed defective.

Within the theory, no later cast can occupy the same relational-temporal marker as an earlier cast. Even repeated visible inputs occur after different interactions, observer states, lineage, entropy conditions, and field context.

Therefore:

- two casts should not be forced to produce identical glyphs
- stochastic variation may be a legitimate expression of temporal uniqueness
- randomness may participate in entropy, harmonic drift, boundary interaction, or unresolved field variables
- recording the generated result matters more than reconstructing an identical artifact from the same visible inputs

The correct engineering goal is not deterministic sameness. It is causal transparency sufficient to understand which portions were sourced, transformed, returned, inherited, and fluidly generated.

Do not introduce fixed seeding as an architectural default before the full mathematics is reviewed.
