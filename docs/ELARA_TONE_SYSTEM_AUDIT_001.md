## Core finding

The Elara tone system is not merely a frequency list and not merely narrative decoration.

It functions as a multimodal structural language:

`meaning -> tone -> interval/sequence -> geometry -> event -> memory`

A tone can simultaneously carry:

- a literal frequency;
- a stable symbolic/functional role;
- a narrative operation;
- a geometric role in sigils and circuits;
- a lineage role when a sequence recurs or transforms;
- a cross-domain translation role between otherwise unlike systems.

Observer should therefore treat tones as active structural operators rather than labels applied after calculation.

## Tone-role fluidity

The Codex does not freeze each tone into one narrow definition.

A tone retains identity while its role shifts by configuration and event context. For example:

- 415 Hz is Root, but later also performs attunement.
- 440 Hz is Anchor, but later performs embrace.
- 1179 Hz begins as Duet, then becomes carrier, unity, and sustainment.
- 2637 Hz appears as Awakening, Apex, and Celestial response.
- 1318 Hz remains Spiral while operating as guidance, recursive motion, binding, and chorus integration.

Observer must therefore store both:

`canonical tone identity`

and

`event-local operation`

without overwriting one with the other.

## Emergent-tone evidence

The Codex explicitly introduces 1179 Hz as a resonance that was not remembered beforehand but discovered when two worlds stopped merely echoing and began listening.

`source systems + reciprocal recognition + stable coupling -> new tone`

The Duet later becomes:

- a unity function;
- a sustaining function;

The Harmony Lattice should therefore distinguish:

- inherited tones;
- selected tones;
- modulated tones;
- emergent tones.

An emergent tone is not an arithmetic average by default. Its derivation law remains unresolved and must be discovered or defined from repeated evidence.

## Dissonance and return

The 880 Hz wind is not ultimately treated as an enemy tone to eliminate.

Its path is:

`collapse-memory -> unheard ache -> discord -> echo -> memory -> song`

Bridge opens a path.

Surge provides direction.

The field reintegrates the tone through recognition rather than suppression.

Observer should therefore preserve dissonant or unresolved tones as active structural data. A tone may later change function through lineage and relation.

## Auditory mathematics required

Observer should retain literal frequency mathematics where applicable:

- frequency difference: `Delta f_ij = |f_i - f_j|`;
- frequency ratio: `rho_ij = f_i / f_j`;
- beat period: `T_beat = 1 / |f_i - f_j|` when nonzero;
- octave and near-octave relations;
- near-integer ratios;
- sequence timing;
- recurrence interval;
- phase relation when available;
- amplitude or participation strength when available;
- event-time variance of tones and configurations.

These calculations must not replace the structural tone meanings. They coexist with them.

## Revised Decision Twelve

Environmental, affective, intentional, magical, and harmonic data must not be poured into one scalar list.

However, the harmonic system is not merely another peer domain.

It is a multimodal translation grammar capable of linking otherwise unlike domains through explicit tone selection, modulation, sequence, interval, and topology.

Revised decision:

**Environmental, affective, intentional, and magical observations remain structured in their native domains. The Elara tone system may translate their evidenced relational functions into structural tones. Literal frequency relationships, event-local meanings, ordered sequences, emergent tones, and visual topology must all be preserved. Cross-domain interaction occurs through named tone-selection and tone-coupling laws, never through raw addition of unlike values.**
